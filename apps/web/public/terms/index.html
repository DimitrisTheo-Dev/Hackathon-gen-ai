Terms of use

The terms for using Faceful while it is in development.

Faceful is a development-stage AI video studio. By signing in to the studio, connecting an agent or connecting a social account, you agree to these terms. They will be replaced by reviewed terms before Faceful is offered generally; the date of the current version is 15 November 2026.

What Faceful does

Faceful helps you plan short videos, make them with an approved likeness, and post them to social accounts you connect. Agents you connect may draft campaigns and schedules. Only a signed-in person can approve a schedule, and nothing is posted to a social account until that approval. Features, limits and availability can change while the project is in development, and the live status shows what this deployment has switched on.

Your account and workspace

Keep your sign-in to yourself and only connect agents and tools you trust. You are responsible for what is done in your workspace under your account, including what agents you connected draft for you. Do not try to reach another workspace's material, get around the approval step, or interfere with the service or other people's use of it.

Likeness and your material

You may only make videos of a person's face or voice with that person's own recorded consent, given in the studio. You keep the rights you have in the material you upload. You allow Faceful to store and process it to make the videos you ask for, and you confirm you have the right to use it that way. Do not use Faceful to impersonate anyone, to mislead people about who is speaking, or to make content that is unlawful, harassing or sexual. Videos made with AI are marked as AI-generated wherever the platform offers a label.

Connected social accounts

When you connect a TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or other account, you allow Faceful to post to it only what you approved, at the time you approved, with the visibility you chose. You can disconnect an account at any time in the studio, or remove Faceful's access in the platform's own settings. Each platform's own terms and community guidelines apply to what is posted there, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Music Usage Confirmation for videos posted to TikTok. Faceful keeps a platform's access only to post and to confirm what happened to a post, stores it encrypted, and does not sell it or use it for anything else.

Development stage

Faceful is provided as it is, without promises of availability, accuracy or fitness for a purpose, to the extent the law allows. Check every video before you approve it: you are responsible for what you approve and post. Faceful may suspend access that breaks these terms or puts other people at risk.

Questions

Read how data is handled in the privacy notes. Use the contact guidance for questions about these terms or to ask for an account and its material to be removed.
